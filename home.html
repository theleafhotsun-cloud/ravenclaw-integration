    Welcome to RMIS, Staff!

    The Ravenclaw Management Information System (RMIS) is the central command center for our House operations. From tracking student activities to securing official guidelines, this platform empowers our divisions to work seamlessly, transparently, and efficiently.

    🦅 Technical Guide & Module Overview

    RMIS is meticulously designed as an integrated and automated administrative platform. It bridges coordination among the Head of House, Prefects, Vice Prefects, Lead Rooms, and Editors, ensuring high accountability across all management levels.

    Core Modules Guide:

    • 📝
      Task Master: Your personal task tracking dashboard. Check the box (✓) upon task completion. Monthly KPI reports can be submitted directly from this module.
    • 📚
      Documents & Guidelines: The central repository for all official manuals. Document access is strictly restricted based on your administrative role's clearance level.
    • 📊
      Active Room Database: Automatically tracks room attendance, summaries, dues, and penalties. Exclusive for Leadrooms and P-VPs to control and evaluate room activities.
    • 📖
      Library & Class Database: The center for class mapping, assignments, and learning materials for all staff to ensure educational accessibility.
    • 💰
      House Finance: A comprehensive module for tracking cash flows, penalties (fines), and monthly rewards/salaries in real-time.
    • 🏆
      Rank Panel: Automated house point calculation to determine the Best Room title fairly and transparently based on cumulative room performance.
