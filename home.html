    🦅 Ravenclaw Management Integration System (RMIS)

    The Ravenclaw Management Integration System (RMIS) serves as the central platform for Ravenclaw House operations. It brings together the House's administrative, operational, academic, financial, and management needs within a single integrated system. RMIS is designed to support seamless, transparent, and efficient coordination across the House while maintaining clear accountability at every level of management.

    🔐 Role-Based Account System

    RMIS uses a Role-Based Account System, meaning each account is configured according to the user's assigned role and responsibilities within Ravenclaw House. Access to features, data, and administrative functions may therefore differ between roles such as the Head of House, Prefects, Vice Prefects, Lead Rooms, Editors, and other authorised users. Each account is provided only with the level of access required for its respective responsibilities.

    🛠️ Access & Technical Notes

    RMIS can be accessed through:
    ravenclaw-integration.vercel.app/index.html

    • 👤
      Users should sign in using the username and password assigned to their respective account. Accounts are personal and must not be shared or used by another person.
    • 💾
      RMIS uses Local Storage, meaning certain data and session information are stored locally within the user's browser. As a result, the browser may occasionally retain outdated system data or files after an update.
    • 🔄
      For this reason, users are advised to refresh the page or use Ctrl + F5 when first accessing RMIS, after a system update, or whenever a feature does not appear or function as expected.

    ⚠️ Login & Access Issues

    If you are unable to log in or encounter an unexpected system issue:

    • 1.
      Check that your username and password are entered correctly.
    • 2.
      Refresh the page or use Ctrl + F5.
    • 3.
      If the issue persists, clear the RMIS cache, cookies, or site data. This removes outdated browser data that may interfere with the latest version of the system.
    • 4.
      Alternatively, access RMIS through Incognito/Private Mode to test the system using a fresh browser session.
    • 5.
      If the issue remains unresolved, report it to the Head of House with your account role and a brief description of the problem.

    Important: Never share your password when reporting an issue.
